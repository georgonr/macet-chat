## Full description (max 4000 characters)

```
Macet Secure Chat is a messenger for people who want to know where their messages go.

ENCRYPTION
The content of messages, files and calls is end-to-end encrypted between the
participants' devices. The server operator only ever sees encrypted data and cannot read
it. That covers file names and profile names you exchange with your contacts as well.

SELF-HOSTED SERVERS
The app connects only to servers run on our own infrastructure (cht.macet.eu) — for
message delivery, for file transfer and for relaying calls. It talks to no third-party
servers.

NO ACCOUNT
Creating a profile takes no phone number, no email address and no password. The profile
name is local data you share only with your contacts. You connect to someone through a
one-time link or a QR code.

NO ADS, NO TRACKING
The app contains no ads, no analytics, no telemetry and no crash reporting.

WHAT THE SERVER DOES SEE
Encrypted content does not mean invisible. The server sees the IP addresses of connecting
devices, connection times and the volume of transferred data. To limit that, you can turn
on the SOCKS proxy in the settings and route traffic over Tor. The privacy policy spells
this out in full.

DATA RETENTION
The servers keep no message history — a message is removed as soon as it is delivered.
Undelivered messages are deleted after 21 days, files and media after 48 hours. Your
conversation history lives only in the encrypted database on your device; if you lose it,
it cannot be restored.

CALLS
Audio and video calls go through a TURN relay, which sees the duration and volume of a
call but not its content. "Always use relay" is on by default; if you turn it off, the
call is established directly and the other party will see your IP address.

MORE FEATURES
• groups, replies, editing and deleting messages
• sending photos, videos, files and voice messages
• disappearing messages
• several profiles in one app
• app lock with a passcode or biometrics
• encrypted local database
• light and dark themes
• a desktop build for Windows

OPEN SOURCE
Macet Secure Chat is a fork of SimpleX Chat, distributed under the AGPLv3. The source of
this build is public and the build can be verified against it:
https://github.com/georgonr/macet-chat
```

## Notes for the Data safety form

- The app collects no data for analytics or advertising.
- Message, file and call content is encrypted in transit and at rest (end-to-end).
- No identifiers are collected: no phone number, no email address, no device ID.
- The server processes the IP address and connection metadata as technically required to
  deliver a message; it does not retain them as a user profile.
- Users can have their data deleted by uninstalling the app — all data is local to the
  device.
